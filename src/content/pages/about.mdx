I'm a machine learning engineer in Munich. Since 2023 I've worked at Semorai, a German software
company with automotive and manufacturing enterprise clients, on an LLM document-intelligence
platform. I built the first version of its requirement-extraction service and was the primary
developer of the rewrite — about half of all commits, most of the integration and release work,
219 automated releases. I co-developed the shared LLM gateway every service on the platform calls
through.

Outside work I'm co-founder and CTO of Olber, a services marketplace in Uzbekistan with a
real-money escrow wallet, live on both app stores with over ten thousand registered users. Three
of us built and operate it in evenings and weekends.

## How I work

I start from production output, not from the design doc. Most of what I'm proud of began as a
root-cause analysis of something wrong in a real result — a standards code spelled four ways, a
citation nobody could open, a stream that died at exactly sixty seconds. The fix usually made the
model do less: deterministic code first, the model last, and only asked the one question it's good
at.

I write a lot of tests — 21,000 lines into one suite — because the systems I build run for minutes
and fail in ways a unit test can't see. I'd rather ship a hedged number than an impressive one. And
I like being the person who integrates other people's work and cuts the release.
